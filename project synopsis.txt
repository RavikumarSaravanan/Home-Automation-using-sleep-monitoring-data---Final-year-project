27ITPS005 – IoT-Powered Adaptive Sleep Environment for Health & Wellness

SYNOPSIS
1. TITLE OF THE PROJECT
IoT-Powered Adaptive Sleep Environment for Health & Wellness
2. DOMAIN
Internet of Things (IoT)
Healthcare Technology
Smart Home Systems
3. PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION / INTRODUCTION
Sleep quality is a critical component of human health and wellness, directly influencing physical fitness, mental health, and daily productivity. Poor sleep patterns may lead to chronic health conditions such as stress, anxiety, cardiovascular diseases, and reduced cognitive performance. Traditional sleep monitoring techniques like polysomnography provide accurate results but are costly, intrusive, and unsuitable for continuous home-based monitoring.
Most existing smart home systems focus mainly on energy efficiency and static appliance control rather than dynamically optimizing sleep conditions based on real-time physiological data. These systems lack the ability to monitor biometric signals such as heart rate, motion, and breathing patterns and fail to adapt environmental conditions accordingly.
Hence, there is a need for a cost-effective, IoT-based adaptive sleep environment that continuously monitors biometric parameters, learns individual sleep patterns, and intelligently adjusts environmental factors to enhance sleep quality.
4. PROBLEM STATEMENT
To design and develop an IoT-powered adaptive sleep environment that monitors real-time biometric signals such as heart rate, motion, and breathing patterns, analyzes sleep behavior, and dynamically optimizes environmental parameters like temperature, lighting, airflow, and noise levels to improve sleep quality and overall health.
5. OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
To monitor real-time biometric parameters using IoT sensors
To analyze sleep patterns and generate personalized sleep profiles
To integrate smart home devices for automated environmental control
To dynamically optimize sleep conditions using intelligent decision logic
To predict sleep disturbances and provide alerts or recommendations
6. PROPOSED SOLUTION / METHODOLOGY
The proposed system consists of IoT sensors connected to a microcontroller that continuously collects biometric and environmental data during sleep. The collected data is processed locally and stored in the cloud for analysis. Intelligent algorithms analyze sleep patterns and determine optimal environmental conditions.
Based on the analysis, the system automatically controls smart devices such as lights, fans, and temperature controllers through relay modules. Predictive logic is used to detect possible sleep disturbances and take proactive actions or generate alerts. The system gradually learns individual sleep behavior to improve accuracy and personalization over time.
7. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (BRIEF)
Input Layer: Heart rate sensor, PIR motion sensor, temperature & humidity sensor
Processing Layer: ESP32 / Arduino microcontroller, decision logic
Output Layer: Smart light, fan / AC control via relay
Cloud Layer: Data storage, monitoring dashboard

14. LITERATURE REVIEW (BRIEF)
Several researchers have proposed IoT-based sleep monitoring systems using low-cost sensors and machine learning techniques. Saleem et al. presented a smart and cost-effective IoT healthcare system for sleep quality monitoring using ambient and physiological parameters. Gamel and Talaat proposed an IoT-enabled continual learning approach for intelligent sleep enhancement. Cong Thuan Do et al. introduced an IoT-powered healthcare system using EEG signals for sleep state analysis. These works highlight the importance of IoT and AI in sleep monitoring while indicating the need for adaptive environmental optimization, which is addressed in the proposed system.
