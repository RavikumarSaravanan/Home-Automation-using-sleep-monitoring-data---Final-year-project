1. Project Abstract

This project presents an intelligent, interconnected ecosystem designed to optimize personal health and energy conservation. By pairing an edge-sensing node (ESP32) with a central automation server (Raspberry Pi 4) over a lightweight messaging protocol (MQTT), the system tracks real-time biological vitals and environmental conditions during sleep cycles.
The system acts as a closed-loop automation platform: when biometric sensors indicate a user has transitioned from awake to sleep states, the central server automatically modifies the physical environment—dimming primary light fixtures, activating soft night-lamps, and adjusting the fan speed dynamically according to the room’s ambient temperature.
